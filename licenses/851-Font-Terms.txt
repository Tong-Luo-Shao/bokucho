The font ships with no license file of its own; its terms are published on the
author's site at the addresses above, in Japanese, and those pages are the
authoritative text. In summary, as of the version Bokucho ships (0.883):

- The author keeps the copyright, and nobody may claim to have made the font.
- Use is free for both non-commercial and commercial work, with no permission
  or credit required. The author lists embedding in software, games and images
  among the permitted uses.
- Modifying and redistributing the font are freely allowed.
- The font may not be sold on its own. A modified font file may be sold only
  after asking the author first.
- The font comes with no warranty; the author takes no responsibility for any
  damage arising from its use.

Bokucho redistributes the unmodified file, renamed 851tegaki_zatsu.ttf, so that
pictures can be typeset in it. It is not sold.
